HTTP CodeErrorWhen

403ForbiddenJWT valid but role does not have permission for this resource.
404Not FoundResource does not exist or does not belong to caller's branch.
409ConflictDuplicate mobile on lead/patient, double-booking of appointment slot.

429Rate LimitedToo many requests. Retry-After header included.
