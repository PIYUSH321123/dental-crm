PATIENT MOBILE APP — FEATURE LIST
Sales Guru CRM & Telehealth Platform

======================================
HOME / OVERVIEW
======================================
- Next session card (date, therapist, join button if telehealth)
- Adherence/progress snapshot (exercise completion streak, pain score trend)
- Quick actions: Book consultation, Pay dues, Message therapist

======================================
CONSULTATION
======================================
- Book new consultation (select clinic, therapist/specialist, date & time slot)
- Consultation type selector (in-person / telehealth video call)
- Reason for visit / symptoms note (submitted before session)
- Upcoming consultations list (reschedule / cancel)
- Past consultation history with shared summary notes
- Join video call button (for telehealth consultations)
- Consultation reminders/notifications

======================================
MY SESSIONS
======================================
- Upcoming therapy/treatment sessions
- Past session history with therapist notes (patient-visible only)
- Session status (upcoming, completed, missed, cancelled)
- Reschedule / cancel options
- Reminders toggle

======================================
MY PROGRESS
======================================
- Exercise prescriptions with instructions/video demos
- Daily/weekly adherence tracker (checkbox or streak view)
- Pain/mobility score trend chart over time
- Goals set by therapist, visible to patient

======================================
SUBSCRIPTION
======================================
- View active subscription plan (e.g. Monthly / Quarterly / Pay-per-session)
- Plan details: sessions included, consultations included, validity period
- Upgrade / downgrade / renew plan
- Auto-renewal toggle
- Subscription usage tracker (e.g. "5 of 8 sessions used")
- Plan comparison view (if multiple tiers offered)
- Cancel subscription option

======================================
BILLING
======================================
- Invoice history
- Outstanding dues with in-app payment (UPI/card)
- Payment method management
- Linked to subscription plan billing cycle

======================================
MESSAGES
======================================
- Chat with assigned therapist/clinic (WhatsApp integration)
- Consultation & session-related notifications
- Reminders for reschedules and follow-ups

======================================
PROFILE / SETTINGS
======================================
- Personal details, emergency contact
- Notification preferences
- Linked clinic(s) if patient sees multiple providers
- Document uploads (reports for therapist review)

======================================
EXCLUDED (staff-only, must NOT appear in patient app)
======================================
- Internal/staff notes
- Lead-loss reasons
- Other patients' data
- Admin/feature-flag controls
- Raw clinical assessment data (only patient-friendly summaries shown)
